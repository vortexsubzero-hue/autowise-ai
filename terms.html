Early beta

AutoWise AI is an experimental vehicle-information service. Features may change, be limited, or be unavailable. These draft terms are intended for beta testing and require legal review plus operator contact details before a public paid launch.

Not professional automotive advice

AutoWise provides general educational information, not a confirmed diagnosis, inspection, repair authorization, warranty, or guarantee. AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Have safety-critical concerns inspected by a qualified technician. Stop driving and seek appropriate help if a vehicle has unsafe braking or steering, smoke or fire, a strong fuel smell, severe overheating, or another immediate hazard.

Vehicle and recall data

Vehicle specifications and recall research may come from NHTSA services. A successful VIN decode does not prove ownership, condition, title history, maintenance history, or recall eligibility. Model-based recall results must be confirmed for the exact VIN through NHTSA, the manufacturer, or an authorized dealer.

  • Use the service only where lawful and make your own safety and repair decisions with qualified help.
